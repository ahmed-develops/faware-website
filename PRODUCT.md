### Summary

Faware is an agentic AI operating system built for independent restaurant operators who struggle with disconnected systems, razor-thin margins, and operational burnout. Rather than forcing teams to manually monitor static dashboards, Faware delivers complete front-to-back operational autonomy through a natively integrated core software paired with an open Model Context Protocol (MCP) server layer.

At the heart of the platform are dedicated Front of House and Back of House automation engines. For front-of-house operations, Faware effortlessly automates table management, reservations, and customer communications to maintain optimal floor pacing without host fatigue. In the back of house, smart AI agents streamline live inventory, automate supplier purchase orders, and optimize daily kitchen prep against real-time sales velocity. To elevate kitchen operations further, Faware includes a personalized in-built chef platform, empowering culinary teams with customized tools tailored directly to their unique kitchen workflows and station setups.

To bridge existing venue infrastructure, the system features a dedicated Model Context Protocol (MCP) server. This layer seamlessly connects and communicates with your existing POS and management software, exposing live sales feeds, inventory levels, and operational resources to AI agents without requiring operators to abandon their legacy software investments.

Every tier includes full access to all administrative capabilities, ensuring independent operators never face paywalled features or surprise add-on fees to run their business. Operating headlessly through lightweight mobile action cards for human-in-the-loop approvals, Faware unifies existing hospitality tools into an autonomous, proactive execution loop.

### Feature Set

#### Back of house operations (BackHQ):
- **Live Inventory Reconciliation:** Ingests real-time sales and modifier data via the MCP server to continuously calculate stock depletion against expected recipe yields.
- **Predictive Supplier Purchase Orders:** Evaluates historical burn rates, upcoming reservations, and lead times to automatically generate wholesale purchase orders across verified distributor accounts.
- **Dynamic Shift Trimming:** Monitors live labor-to-sales ratios against revenue pacing, generating lightweight mobile action cards for floor managers to release casual staff before penalty rates hit.
- **Delivery App Pacing:** Reads real-time kitchen line capacity and automatically pauses, throttles, or adjusts prep times on external delivery platforms to protect in-house food quality.
- **Invoice & Delivery Verification:** Automatically compares incoming supplier delivery dockets against original purchase orders and billed invoices to catch short-ships, off-spec cuts, and unwarranted price increases.
- **Automated Compliance Logging:** Collects digital HACCP logs, food temperature checks, and kitchen sanitation tasks via scheduled agent prompts without manual paper checklists.

#### Front of house operations (FrontHQ):
- **Autonomous Table Pacing & Turn Optimization:** Syncs floor status with POS table states via the MCP server to dynamically adjust seating windows and maximize seat utilization during peak service.
- **Smart Reservation Orchestration:** Coordinates incoming booking requests across phone, web, and walk-ins, balancing party sizes against live kitchen throughput to prevent station bottlenecks.
- **Automated Two-Way Guest Communication:** Handles booking confirmations, dietary requirement checks, arrival delays, and deposit verifications autonomously over SMS and WhatsApp.
- **Waitlist Flow Management:** Calculates dynamic quote times based on live course progression and table turnover velocity, notifying waiting guests with accurate return estimates.
- **Headless Mobile Action Cards:** Dispatches single-tap approval prompts to front-of-house leads for VIP table releases, large party holds, and late cancellations without requiring a dedicated terminal.

#### Autonomous Sous Chef (ChefHQ):
- **Interactive Dashboard & Chat:** High-level operations overview coupled with an integrated AI chat interface for rapid knowledge access.
- **Recipe & Menu Planning:** Dedicated tools for crafting culinary recipes and planning upcoming menus.
- **Live Inventory Management:** Comprehensive inventory tracking featuring a Live Cooking Panel, Baskets View, and an Add Items Wizard.
- **Automated Shopping List:** Real-time tracking of required ingredients and streamlined supplier order generation.
- **Vendor & HQ Workflow:** Integrated management of vendor APIs and centralized operations workflows.
- **AI Context Configuration:** Interface to govern context and operational parameters for backend AI agents.
- **Team Management:** Tools to oversee culinary crew profiles, ranks, and system notifications.
